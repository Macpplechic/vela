# Vela Wellness – Support

For help with the Vela Wellness app, please contact us at:

**Email:** support@velaforwomen.com

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, and select Vela Wellness to manage or cancel.

**How do I restore a previous purchase?**
Tap "Restore purchase" on the CoolDown or FluxLog subscription screen inside the app.

**I found a bug – how do I report it?**
Email us at support@velaforwomen.com with a description of the issue and your device model.

**How do I delete my data?**
Email support@velaforwomen.com with your request and we will delete your data within 30 days.
