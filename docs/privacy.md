# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Information We Collect
Vela stores all health data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal health information to our servers.

## Data Storage
All symptom logs, food entries, sleep data, and cycle information are stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage.

## Third-Party Services
- **RevenueCat**: Processes subscription payments. Subject to RevenueCat's privacy policy.
- **Apple**: App Store purchases subject to Apple's privacy policy.
- **USDA FoodData Central**: Food search queries are sent to the USDA API (no personal data included).

## Your Rights
You may delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the App.
